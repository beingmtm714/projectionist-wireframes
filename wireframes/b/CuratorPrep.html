CONSEQUENCE OF THE NEW SEQUENCE

The spec has house calls at T-10 and T-1. The two-minute notification replaces the T-1 call, and it should: at T-1 the curtain appears, and that is a better one-minute call than a notification. It is visible, it needs no permission, and it cannot be missed by someone already looking at the screen.

Calls become T-10 and T-2. The curtain makes the last call itself.

CHOREOGRAPHY 1 — THE CHAT DROP

At T-0:20 two things move at once: chat goes from full screen to a strip, and the curtain begins to part behind it. Together they read as a glitch.

Settle the chat first, over about a second, and begin the parting as it lands. One cue, then the next, rather than both at once.

CHOREOGRAPHY 2 — CURTAIN TRAVEL

Twenty to fifteen is five seconds for a full curtain travel. Real curtains take eight to twelve, and a fast curtain reads as cheap in a way that is hard to name and easy to feel.

Start the part at T-0:25 so the movement is unhurried and still lands fully open at T-0:15, when the leader begins.

WHAT THE PAIR IS TESTING

Whether two lighting states can carry the whole system where three temperatures used to.

Squint at both boards. House up should read as the same building as house down, at a different time of night. If either reads as a different place, the model is wrong and no amount of token tuning fixes it.
