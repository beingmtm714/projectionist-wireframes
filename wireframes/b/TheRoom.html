One cold key from above and behind, the way a projector sits over your shoulder. Everything falls off toward the bottom of the screen.
Chat lives in the deepest part of that falloff, which is right: it is the murmur of the room. When a note fires the beam moves onto it, chat drops to a strip, and the note is the brightest thing on the surface because it is the thing being lit.
No grain here. The film is running on the television; this is a companion, not a projection. Grain belongs to projected light only.
